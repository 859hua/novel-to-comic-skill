# Reveal Methods

Product reveal determines how ad-like the video feels.

## Guiding Rule

The product should feel like an answer, not an interruption.

## Reveal Types

### 1. Direct hero reveal

How it works:

- product appears clearly early
- classic macro or hero framing

Use when:

- the user wants a conventional ad
- selling clarity matters more than disguise

Risk:

- viewers identify it as an ad immediately

### 2. Result-first reveal

How it works:

- show the change first
- reveal the product after the effect is understood

Use when:

- the outcome is more visually attractive than the object

### 3. Functional reveal

How it works:

- product solves a problem inside the scene
- only after the action does the audience recognize the product

Use when:

- you want smooth integration
- the product has a strong practical role

### 4. Prop reveal

How it works:

- product appears as an ordinary object inside the story world
- its identity as "the ad product" is delayed

Use when:

- story disguise is the main strategy

### 5. Module reveal

How it works:

- product is embedded in a larger system, device, or ritual setup

Use when:

- action, sci-fi, tool, or system logic is involved

### 6. Core reveal

How it works:

- the product is the core of the world logic, weapon logic, or reset logic
- viewers first perceive a force, field, blade, shield, ritual, or mechanism
- later they realize the product is the core

Use when:

- you want the least ad-like integration
- the product's ability maps cleanly to clearing, restoring, shielding, or resetting

### 7. World-rule reveal

How it works:

- a white field, weather event, purge, ritual, signal reset, or narrative mechanism operates through the whole film
- final frames or a carefully timed beat reveal the product as the origin point

Use when:

- you want strong high-concept or VFX-driven storytelling

## Best Reveal Matches

### Conventional functional ads

- direct hero reveal
- result-first reveal

### Story disguise

- prop reveal
- functional reveal

### Action-integrated or Seedance-heavy work

- module reveal
- core reveal

### Premium emotional ads

- result-first reveal
- world-rule reveal

## Anti-Patterns

- protagonist suddenly stops and takes out the product with no story need
- hard cut from drama to macro pack shot too early
- product appears as a brand asset rather than a world object
- reveal timing breaks rhythm instead of rewarding attention
