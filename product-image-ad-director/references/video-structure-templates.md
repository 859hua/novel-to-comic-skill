# Video Structure Templates

Use these as backbone patterns. Default to 15 seconds unless the user asks for another length.

## Universal Rule

A short ad should usually have 3 clean phases:

1. hook or disturbance
2. product logic enters
3. payoff and reveal

## Template A: Story Disguise 15s

Best for:

- making the video feel less like an ad

Timing:

- `0-4s`: enter the wrong genre
- `4-8s`: escalate the scene or problem
- `8-12s`: product logic quietly solves the problem
- `12-15s`: reveal and emotional landing

Good reveal types:

- prop
- functional
- world-rule

## Template B: Premium Sensory 15s

Best for:

- prestige products
- perfume
- high-end skincare

Timing:

- `0-3s`: set atmosphere
- `3-7s`: texture or tension
- `7-11s`: transformation or reveal of effect
- `11-15s`: stillness and hero landing

Good reveal types:

- result-first
- direct hero

## Template C: Pseudo-Program 15s

Best for:

- social-native formats
- clever commentary

Timing:

- `0-2.5s`: recognizable format hook
- `2.5-6s`: explain the "issue"
- `6-10s`: introduce the fix naturally
- `10-15s`: finish with a memorable line or format-specific sign-off

Good reveal types:

- functional
- prop

## Template D: Action-Integrated 15s

Best for:

- products that can map to clearing, resetting, shielding, or restoring control

Timing:

- `0-3s`: conflict or pursuit
- `3-7s`: pressure rises
- `7-10s`: product logic activates inside the action
- `10-13s`: control returns or reversal
- `13-15s`: delayed recognition or core reveal

Good reveal types:

- module
- core

## Template E: Emotional Ritual 15s

Best for:

- end-of-day, reset, recovery, self-return

Timing:

- `0-4s`: show the weight of the day
- `4-8s`: quiet preparation
- `8-12s`: release and return
- `12-15s`: clean emotional resolve

Good reveal types:

- result-first
- prop

## Template F: VFX Spectacle 15s

Best for:

- strong visual contrasts
- high-concept campaigns

Timing:

- `0-3s`: impossible image or anomaly
- `3-7s`: scale up the visual logic
- `7-11s`: product logic reorganizes the chaos
- `11-15s`: collapse into clarity and reveal

Good reveal types:

- core
- world-rule

## Seedance-Specific Control Notes

For Seedance-driven execution:

- use 4 to 6 main shots, not 12 tiny fragments
- keep one primary subject per segment when possible
- use one main camera move per segment
- attach VFX to contact points or environmental reactions
- write sound into the plan, not as an afterthought

## Script-Stage Rule

Once the user confirms a direction, use the matching template but customize:

- product role
- reveal type
- emotional tempo
- platform behavior
