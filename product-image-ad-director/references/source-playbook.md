# Source Playbook

Use public web research before proposing concepts. The user should not need to hand-feed market context.

## Research Goal

Build a practical product-and-market read from one product image.

You are trying to learn:

- what the product most likely is
- what the product most likely claims
- how the category usually advertises itself
- what language real buyers use
- what is overused
- what is working now in high-creativity AI-video or short-video contexts

## Minimum Source Mix

Use at least one source from each bucket when available:

1. official
- brand homepage
- product page
- official launch or campaign page

2. commerce
- ecommerce listing
- marketplace listing
- retailer listing

3. market language
- reviews
- product descriptions
- category explainers

4. creative references
- competitor ads
- same-category campaigns
- public social discussion
- public high-engagement or highly shared creative examples when visible

5. Seedance execution references
- ByteDance Seed official pages
- public community prompt discussions
- public guides or tests
- public Seedance ad or cinematic-example discussions with visible engagement or repeat sharing

6. anti-reference
- weak ads
- low-quality templated videos
- overused category tropes
- low-engagement or obviously generic Seedance-style outputs to avoid

## Search Ladder

### Step 1: Search what is visible

Start with:

- visible brand name
- visible product name
- visible category terms
- translated variants if the package is bilingual or Chinese-only

### Step 2: Search the category if identity is unclear

If the exact product is unclear, search:

- shape + packaging + likely category
- dominant claim words
- likely audience and category words

Example query patterns:

- `brand + product name + official`
- `brand + product name + review`
- `brand + product name + product page`
- `product category + ad campaign`
- `product category + commercial`
- `product category + short video ad`
- `Seedance 2.0 + product category + ad`
- `Seedance 2.0 + product category + commercial`
- `Seedance 2.0 + ad + reddit`
- `Seedance 2.0 + ad + x`
- `Seedance 2.0 + prompt + commercial`

### Step 3: Search for what to avoid

Always search at least one "bad example" angle:

- `product category + ad cringe`
- `product category + generic commercial`
- `product category + overused ad`
- `Seedance 2.0 + common mistakes`

The goal is not to mock bad work. The goal is to map the stale patterns you should not repeat.

## Capture Template

For your internal notes, capture:

- product identity confidence
- brand tone
- likely category promise
- likely buyer
- current category visual cliches
- current category emotional cliches
- same-category good ideas
- same-category overused ideas
- visible traction or repeat-sharing signals when available
- Seedance-specific execution notes

## Source Priorities

- Prefer official and primary sources for product facts.
- Prefer recent sources for current ad patterns and Seedance practices.
- Prefer visible evidence over vague marketing claims.
- Use exact dates for unstable facts or current trend claims.

## Do Not

- do not rely only on the package appearance
- do not propose concepts before checking the market
- do not copy competitor executions directly
- do not use only one source family
- do not treat a single public post as proof of broad effectiveness
- do not confuse visible novelty with product fit
