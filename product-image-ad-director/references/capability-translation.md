# Capability Translation

Most weak product-image ad concepts fail because they stay literal. This file turns product promises into dramatic abilities.

## Rule

Do not stop at:

- cleaning
- hydrating
- long-lasting
- refreshing
- smoothing

Translate the product into what it *does inside a story or world*.

## Translation Table

### Cleansing / removing / dissolving

Possible dramatic abilities:

- clear contamination
- remove attachment
- reset a noisy state
- restore clean vision
- strip away false layers
- dissolve a hostile mark
- return the scene to zero

### Hydrating / nourishing / replenishing

Possible dramatic abilities:

- recharge depleted matter
- revive a cracked world
- restore softness or elasticity
- bring color back
- refill lost energy

### Repair / soothing / barrier support

Possible dramatic abilities:

- mend fractures
- rebuild a shield
- stabilize a damaged state
- calm a hostile environment
- return order after stress

### Brightening / revealing / clarifying

Possible dramatic abilities:

- uncover the true layer beneath
- bring the real signal back
- wipe away haze
- turn noise into clarity

### Oil control / mattifying / balancing

Possible dramatic abilities:

- stop runaway surface chaos
- tame a slippery, unstable field
- turn overload into control

### Makeup / transformation / styling

Possible dramatic abilities:

- build a persona
- switch identities
- armor up
- perform confidence on command
- choose a public face

### Makeup removal

Possible dramatic abilities:

- retire a role
- leave the stage
- dismiss today's mask
- peel away a performed self

### Fragrance / scent / atmosphere

Possible dramatic abilities:

### Beverage / food / snack

Possible dramatic abilities:

- restart energy

- punctuate a moment with satisfaction

### Device / gadget / tool

Possible dramatic abilities:

- lock on

### Household cleaner / spray / detergent

Possible dramatic abilities:

## Translation Rule by Tone

### If the desired tone is premium

Favor:

- reveal
- restore
- refine
- return to origin
- remove the unnecessary

### If the desired tone is action

Favor:

- clear contamination
- break the lock
- recover control
- reset status
- shield the subject

### If the desired tone is emotional

Favor:

- come back to self
- leave the day behind
- release pressure
- return to your own signal

### If the desired tone is playful

Favor:

- evict unwanted guests
- fire the useless extras
- sweep the stage
- turn chaos into order

## Do Not

- do not translate every product into combat
- do not force fantasy when ritual or documentary fits better
- do not pick a metaphor that overclaims beyond the product
- do not use a metaphor that only looks cool but does not clarify the product role
