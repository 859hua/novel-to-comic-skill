# Director Grammar

Use this file during script stage. It turns a concept into a directable piece rather than a vague idea.

## Rule

Every detailed script should make decisions in six lanes:

1. character
2. scene
3. camera
4. light and color
5. VFX or practical effects
6. sound and rhythm

If one lane is missing, the script is probably too abstract.

## Character Lane

Define:

- who the viewer is following
- what role the character is in
- what pressure or goal the character has
- what emotional shift occurs by the end

Avoid:

- generic "beautiful woman"
- character with no task
- emotions that jump without cause

## Scene Lane

Define:

- location
- time of day
- environmental pressure
- key surfaces or objects that can react to action or VFX

Good scene logic gives the product something to change:

- noise to quiet
- contamination to clarity
- stage role to real self
- pursuit to control
- disorder to order

## Camera Lane

For each beat, define:

- shot size
- framing
- one main camera move
- why the move helps this beat

Do not stack multiple major camera moves in the same beat.

## Light and Color Lane

Define:

- dominant palette
- key source
- contrast level
- what changes between beginning and end

Typical good transitions:

- mixed noise light -> controlled clean light
- warm clutter -> cold clarity
- oppressive shadow -> soft recovery

## VFX and Practical Effects Lane

Effects must mean something.

Always define:

- what triggers the effect
- where the effect attaches
- what narrative job it performs
- what should remain restrained

Best effect behaviors:

- attached to contact points
- attached to surfaces
- attached to environmental change
- attached to reveal logic

Weak effect behaviors:

- floating for decoration only
- changing colors for no reason
- overpowering the product role

## Sound and Rhythm Lane

Always define:

- environment bed
- performance sounds or foley
- transition or impact sounds
- music behavior
- where to reduce sound for emphasis

Good rhythm usually alternates:

- pressure
- intervention
- release

## Script Quality Check

A script is strong if:

- the hook is visible in the first seconds
- every beat has one clear job
- the reveal feels earned
- the product is part of the logic
- VFX and sound reinforce the same emotional turn
