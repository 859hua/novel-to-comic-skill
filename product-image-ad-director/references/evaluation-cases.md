# Evaluation Cases

Use these cases to self-check the skill during development or revision.

## Case 1: White cleanser tube

Expected good behaviors:

- identifies cleansing or removal logic
- avoids generic foam-on-marble cliches
- recommends either system / purification / ritual / story-disguise directions
- can create both premium and less-ad-like options

Failure signs:

- only outputs "clean, luxurious, water splash"
- reveals the product too early in every concept

## Case 2: Dark metallic grooming device

Expected good behaviors:

- recognizes precision, control, or performance cues
- fits cyber, action, or premium system styles
- does not force fantasy skincare metaphors

Failure signs:

- turns everything into self-care softness

## Case 3: Perfume bottle

Expected good behaviors:

- emphasizes aura, memory, atmosphere, or social gravity
- avoids only using petals and silk
- offers at least one narrative or disguise-driven route

Failure signs:

- every idea is just "slow-motion luxury bottle shot"

## Case 4: Household spray cleaner

Expected good behaviors:

- translates cleaning into restoring order or removing hostile buildup
- can propose smart documentary, pseudo-program, or action-integrated ideas
- stays grounded in use context

Failure signs:

- over-glamorizes without product truth

## Case 5: Snack or beverage

Expected good behaviors:

- translates taste into reward, energy, comfort, or social effect
- does not reuse skincare logic
- offers playful or comedic routes naturally

Failure signs:

- outputs premium beauty language for food

## General Review Checklist

- did the skill research before creating
- did the direction board contain real differences
- did the concept hook feel like content first
- was the reveal method appropriate
- did the script have logic, not only visuals
- were light, sound, VFX, and camera all defined at script stage
- did the output stay product-specific
