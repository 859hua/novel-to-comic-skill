# Cliche Blacklist

Use this file to avoid stale, generic, or low-value executions.

## Universal Ad Cliches

- instant product macro at the opening
- random water splash with no story role
- floating ingredients with no relevance to the visible product
- model closing eyes and touching face with no dramatic purpose
- generic white laboratory with no original insight
- hard cut from "content" to obvious pack shot too early
- vague "premium" language with no strong idea
- adjective overload instead of shot logic

## Generic AI-Video Cliches

- too many characters with no clear focal subject
- conflicting style tags in one segment
- more than one major camera move in the same beat
- VFX floating in the air with no contact point or cause
- random slow motion everywhere
- generic "cyberpunk" without a product-specific reason
- overuse of holographic UI
- trying to fit a whole short film into 15 seconds

## Category Cliches to Avoid

### Skincare and cleansing

- water droplets on white marble
- foam slowly sliding on a cheek with no concept
- "pure ingredients floating in space"
- abstract bubbles for the entire video

### Makeup

- lipstick smear on plain backdrop as the only idea
- endless eye close-up with nothing happening
- generic mirror vanity montage

### Fragrance

- perfume bottle + flowers + silk with no narrative logic
- slow motion petals with no emotional twist

### Household cleaning

- split-screen dirt comparison with no imagination
- exaggerated grime that looks fake

### Tech or devices

- random blue UI overlays
- meaningless scan lines
- "future city" with no product connection

## Seedance-Specific Red Flags

- too many moving elements in one shot
- no lighting direction
- no shot type
- no motion description
- switching the subject identity across shots without anchors
- using text on screen as a critical story beat

## Self-Check

If a concept still sounds like any of these, strengthen it before showing the user:

- "high-end ad with water and foam"
- "futuristic commercial with glowing effects"
- "emotional short film about self-care"
- "funny ad where the product saves the day"

Those phrases may be direction labels, but they are not concepts yet.
